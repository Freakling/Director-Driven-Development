## Pitch

The how-to reference for Director-Driven Development: what the method is, how the loop and its roles work, how to keep token costs down, and how to install and use each of the three frameworks. The blog at saemundsson.se explains why; this site explains how, and each framework's repository stays the source of truth for its procedures.

**Who it's for:** developers and tech leads who direct AI coding agents and can judge the design of code, and readers of the blog who want the reference behind a post. (inferred, please confirm)

**The feeling it should give:** calm, technical and trustworthy: a short, accurate reference in the same dark, one-accent look as the blog figures and LinkedIn cards. (inferred, please confirm)

## Core Flows

### Grasp DDD in one screen
A reader lands on the home page and comes away knowing the four roles, the four steps of the loop and the three frameworks, with a link to read more on each.

### Learn the concept
A reader works through the concept pages (roles, loop, sizing, reviews, align and drift reset) and can explain how a task goes from decision to commit, and when a review or a reset happens.

### Pick and install a framework
A reader compares the frameworks, opens the one that fits, and installs it with one of the listed install paths, knowing what its check enforces and which commands it ships.

### Cut token cost
A reader understands where tokens go, which savings the frameworks build in, and applies the tuning steps to their own sessions.

### Follow a link from the blog
A reader arrives from a saemundsson.se post on the matching docs page, and can get back to the post that explains the why.
